The research hub for **"A Data-Driven Approach to Forecasting Passenger Demand for Improved Public Transport Accessibility in Sri Lanka"** is now live.

This site will serve as the knowledge hub for the IS4101 Final Year Research Project at the University of Colombo School of Computing — tracking progress, documenting methodology, and eventually sharing results with the wider research community.

{/* truncate */}

## What the research is about

Sri Lanka's public transport system — operated by the SLTB alongside private operators — carries the majority of daily commuters. But operational decisions like route planning, fleet allocation, and frequency setting continue to rely on infrequent manual surveys rather than systematic analysis of demand data.

This project builds the predictive foundation that evidence-based planning needs: a data-driven demand forecasting framework that combines AFC ticketing, GPS/AVL vehicle traces, weather data, calendar events, and socio-demographic context to produce accurate short-term and long-term passenger demand forecasts at the route and stop level.
